Organization

Teams, access and follow-through

One Signal Noir, different capabilities for different teams. Decide what each group sees, and keep the record of what was decided.

⚠ Access here shapes the interface. It is not enforced security. Signal Noir runs entirely in this browser, so these checks are made by code the viewer controls and anyone technical can bypass them. Use them to show people the parts of Signal Noir relevant to their job — not to protect confidential data. Real enforcement needs a server that re-checks every request, which Signal Noir does not have yet.
