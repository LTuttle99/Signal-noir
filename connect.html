How do you want to work?

Signal Noir runs entirely on your device unless you choose otherwise. Connecting adds an organization, shared projects and server-enforced access — it does not move anything you have already made.

Sign in to an organization

For teams who need shared projects and real access control.

  • Projects shared with the people you choose
  • Teams and access profiles enforced by the server
  • Reachable from more than one device

No connected workspace is configured for this deployment. Signal Noir is running as a local application. Everything still works; there is simply no server to sign in to.

Signing out later clears your organization session and anything cached from it. It never removes local projects or settings.
